# Planted-gap curriculum: summary (2026-10-22)

The question: which kinds of gaps does GapFinder find? Gaps were planted on papers it already reproduces, and it
searched for them without the paper (`--hypotheses repomap`, no model call). Built by `eval/planted/curriculum.py`
(design in its docstring); the per-task tables are generated by `curriculum.py report`:

- [c1, seed 2](planted_curriculum_c1_seed2.md): engine tag `planted-c1`, 14 gap tasks and 11 controls.
- [c1, seed 3](planted_curriculum_c1_seed3.md) and [c2, seed 3](planted_curriculum_c2_seed3.md): 16 fresh gap
  tasks, made after c2 was frozen, searched by both versions.

These are development measurements. The builder knows every family, and the generator ran on six papers
(pygcn, FastGCN, Fair Clustering, SparseTSF, RippleNet, VGAE). They are not blind evidence.

## What GapFinder finds (c1)

Planted gap tasks, seeds 2 and 3 together:

| Family | Level 1 (>= 5 sd) | Level 2 (3-5 sd) |
|---|---|---|
| Scale or unit convention ("the paper's v is the code's 2 x v") | 4 of 4 | 2 of 3 |
| Setting (a flag or a constant at another value) | 0 of 9 | 2 of 9 |
| Code defect (an arithmetic or reduction edit) | 0 of 3 | 0 of 2 |
| Reporting rule, paper-era code, old package release | not planted | not planted |
| **All** | **4 of 16** | **4 of 14** |

- **Confidently wrong: 0 of 30 gap tasks and 0 of 11 controls.** Every miss carried an unconfident label
  ("Fits but unsupported", "Unexplained" or "Inconclusive"), and every control was reported as reproduced.
- **Scale conventions are found.** The scale probe names the knob and the factor and labels it a paper-artifact
  inconsistency.
- **Settings are mostly not found.** Of the 16 setting misses, 10 come close in at least one way. 6 name the
  planted knob at another value (`hidden1=160` for a planted 4), and 7 have it among the configurations GapFinder
  says the target can't tell apart.
  On pygcn the task gives one number (Cora accuracy only), so several settings fit it equally well. GapFinder says
  so, but the scorer counts it as missed.
- **Code defects are not found.** A code round was cut by the run budget in 19 of the 30 searches (section 7.1 of docs/CONTEXT_v11.md has every failure class). In 4 of the 6 level-1 misses on seed 2, a code round ran out of budget: the patch
  round was one run short of the planted constant (VGAE, twice), and the mutation round never ran (RippleNet,
  FastGCN).
- **Reporting rules, paper-era code and old package releases could not be tested here.** The rebuilt harnesses
  (`eval/planted/rebuild_harnesses.py`) offered these draws on five papers. None moved the target by 3 sd, so no
  such task was made (the rebuilt harnesses produced only controls). Their evidence is still the acceptance toys.
- **The gate was not passed.** Level 1 needs 70% of gap tasks correct; c1 got 25%. Levels 3-5 (two causes,
  adversarial causes outside every family) were not run.

## c2 against c1 on a fresh batch

c2 (`planted-c2`, ca4a5e2) adds `code_hold`. When the code has leads, the patch and mutation rounds' shares are
kept out of the settings exploration. A mutant found after an unsupported fit is reported as an alternative, not
as the answer.

| | c1 | c2 |
|---|---|---|
| Seed 3, level 1 | 3 of 9 | 6 of 9 |
| Seed 3, level 2 | 1 of 7 | 1 of 7 |
| Seed 3, code-defect tasks | 0 of 3 | 0 of 3 |
| Seed 3, confidently wrong | 0 | 0 |
| Dev suite (69 cause runs; c1 = run 5000, c2 = run 6000) | 52 | 48 |
| Dev suite, confidently wrong | 0 | 0 |

- **Seed 3:** c2 is better on 4 tasks and worse on 1 (sign test p = 0.38).
- **Dev suite:** c2 is better on 2 tasks and worse on 6 (p = 0.29).
- **Neither difference is significant, and the gains are not the intended ones.** Every seed-3 gain is a setting
  task; code defects stay at 0 of 3. One gain (pygcn, `lr=0.0025`) was a pick among tied configurations. The
  hold did let the mutation round run (VGAE: 22 mutants tested), but the planted site was not among them. Mutation
  sites are taken from the data flow of the known knobs and the metric code, and `gae/layers.py:30` is on neither.
- **Recommendation:** don't freeze c2 as the next version on this evidence. The next code-defect bottleneck is
  mutation-site coverage, and that fix would need its own fresh batch.

## Infrastructure notes

- The machine lost DNS for minutes at a time. Task making waits for the network and doesn't count draws lost to
  it, and searches resume from `run.cut*` through `--resume`.
- A new worktree needs `.env` copied in, because the file is git-ignored.
- All Sandboxes runs were unbilled, and no model calls were made.
